## Volunteer

1. Open **My shelter** and pick a shelter in your district.
2. **Check-in** tab: fill in name, age, gender, village and special need, then click *Check in*.
3. If the shelter is full, you'll see the nearest shelters that have room.

## Shelter in-charge

- **Evacuees:** search, then *Check out* (with confirmation).
- **Stock:** see days left per item. Record consumption or adjustments.
- **Supply requests:** create a request with several items. When it's DISPATCHED, click *Confirm receipt*; stock goes up once.
- **Facilities:** update medical, ramp and generator.

## District admin

- **District dashboard:** charts, special-needs counts, pending requests, shortages. Filter by event or date.
- **Shelters:** filter by status, create shelters, assign in-charges, export CSV.
- **Supply requests:** approve, reject (with a reason), dispatch.
- **Users:** create volunteers and in-charges.

## State admin

- **Events:** create, activate or deactivate (e.g. "Cyclone Dana 2026 (DEMO)").
- **State dashboard:** district comparison. You can also open any district's dashboard and manage all users.

## Public (no login)

- **Find a relative:** enter the exact name and village. The result shows the shelter name and district only.
- **Available shelters:** shelters with free places, filterable by district.
